## 🚀 Featured Projects

### [EduMate](https://github.com/ductrien261) - Personalized Teaching & Learning Platform 🔒 *Private Source*
**Role:** Team Lead · System Architect · Developer

* **Research & Design:** led the research phase and designed the end-to-end system architecture for a personalized education platform.
* **Knowledge Engineering:** built **Knowledge Graph** systems and workflows to map educational content.
* **Advanced RAG:** hybrid retrieval combining vector search (**Qdrant**) and graph-based retrieval (**Neo4j**) for high-precision context.
* **Infrastructure:** **FastAPI/Django** backends, **Neon** (PostgreSQL) and **Backblaze B2** storage.
* **Tech Stack:** `LangGraph` · `Qdrant` · `Neo4j` · `FastAPI` · `Django` · `Next.js` · `Neon DB`

### [Synapse Lab](https://github.com/ductrien261) - AI-Powered Interactive Learning Ecosystem 🔒 *Private Source*
**Role:** Team Lead · System Architect · Developer

* **One platform, four learning spaces:** step-by-step **algorithm visualization** (71 algorithms), clickable **3D anatomy** models, **STEM labs** (3D assembly & free wiring with electrical checks, a virtual chemistry/physics bench), and **live classrooms** with video, collaborative whiteboard, PDF slides, polls and quizzes.
* **Grounded AI:** a RAG tutor that answers from ingested textbooks with citations, plus learner profiles with mastery tracking and spaced review.
* **Multi-agent lesson generation:** LangGraph agents assemble new STEM lessons from a fixed component library; positions, answers and validity are checked **by code**, and every AI draft goes through **admin review** before it is published.
* **AI gateway:** every model call passes through one gateway with per-user token quotas, request logs and cost tracking.
* **Tech Stack:** `Next.js` · `Django` · `FastAPI` · `LangGraph` · `Postgres + pgvector` · `Redis` · `LiveKit` · `three.js` · `Docker` · `GitHub Actions`

### [Quorum](https://github.com/ductrien261/quorum) - Research-Based Stock Recommendation System 🔬 *Research-based*
**Role:** Researcher & Developer

* **Research to product:** turned a hierarchical deep learning model for stock ranking into a working web app for Vietnamese investors.
* **Daily pipeline:** after each trading session it fetches prices and news, embeds news with PhoBERT, ranks 78 stocks for the next session and fills in realized returns; catches up automatically on missed sessions.
* **Explainable picks:** shows why each stock is ranked where it is by removing one information source at a time, alongside technical indicators and the news the model used.
* **AI assistant:** a Gemini agent with function calling that answers only from the model's own results, streamed to the UI.
* **Tech Stack:** `PyTorch` · `Transformers` · `FastAPI` · `APScheduler` · `Next.js` · `Gemini` · `Docker`

### [SmartSite](https://github.com/ductrien261/smart-site) - Geospatial Site Selection for F&B Businesses 🔬 *Research-based*
**Role:** Researcher & Developer

* **Multi-source spatial data:** Google Maps reviews with sentiment scores, OpenStreetMap POIs, VIIRS night-time lights and population boundaries for Da Nang, Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, aggregated into ~500 m grid cells.
* **Multimodal deep learning:** a Deep & Cross network with a CNN branch over spatial tensors scores each cell's business potential.
* **Decision tools:** interactive grid map, draw-a-zone analysis with rule-based insights, competition dashboards and market-gap detection.
* **Tech Stack:** `TensorFlow` · `GeoPandas` · `FastAPI` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Goong Maps`

### [Foresight Lab](https://github.com/ductrien261/foresight-lab) - Energy Demand Forecasting & Scenario Simulation 📄 *Published in CMES 2026*
**Role:** Co-author & Developer

* Interactive web app built on **DeGNA**, a hybrid STL + GM(1,1) + GWO-ANFIS model for Vietnam's primary energy demand ([paper](https://doi.org/10.32604/cmes.2026.086196)).
* Scenario sliders, supply–demand balance against national plans, and a tool to train the model on your own data, all running **in the browser** via Pyodide.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python` · `Pyodide` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite`

**Also:** [MindCompass](https://github.com/ductrien261/mind-compass) - a DASS-42 mental-health expert system combining fuzzy logic with Prolog forward/backward chaining.
